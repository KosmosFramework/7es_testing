**Subsystem I.3 — External Photon Field Input.** In the case where jet electrons upscatter external photons from the broad-line region, the dusty torus, or the accretion disk, the process is called external Compton. **Subsystem I.4 — Environmental Matter Input (Stellar Winds, Clouds).** Stars penetrating the jet interact with it, and the collision between the stellar wind and the interstellar medium generates material that can mass-load the jet and produce non-thermal emission. ### Element 2: OUTPUT (O) — Multiple Subsystems Identified

The blazar is exceptional in the number and diversity of its output channels. At least six distinct output subsystems were identified.

**Subsystem O.1 — Synchrotron Radiation Output.** Blazars exhibit multifaceted rapid variability across the entire wavelength range, conspicuous high and variable polarization, and powerful gamma-ray emissions. The low-energy SED hump (radio through UV or X-ray) is produced by synchrotron emission from relativistic electrons gyrating in the jet's magnetic field. This is a broadband electromagnetic energy output carrying polarization information about the magnetic field structure.

**Subsystem O.2 — Inverse Compton Radiation Output.** The high-energy SED hump (X-ray through TeV gamma-ray) is produced by inverse Compton scattering — either synchrotron self-Compton (SSC, where electrons upscatter their own synchrotron photons) or external Compton (EC, where electrons upscatter photons from the BLR, dusty torus, or accretion disk). The highest energy emission can result from the Comptonization of infrared photons from the dusty torus, while the bulk of GeV-range emission may be dominated by the Comptonization of radiation from the broad line region. This constitutes a physically distinct output mechanism from synchrotron, operating through different physics (scattering versus gyration).

**Subsystem O.3 — Neutrino Output.** Measurements from astroparticle experiments, such as the 2017 flare associated with the source TXS 0506+056, indicate that blazars act as multimessenger factories producing both radiation and neutrinos. Neutrino emission via proton-photon (pγ) interactions in hadronic or lepto-hadronic jet models constitutes an entirely independent output channel — weakly interacting, carrying energy and information about hadronic acceleration processes but escaping the system essentially unattenuated.

**Subsystem O.4 — Mechanical/Kinetic Jet Output.** The jet itself is a massive outflow of relativistic plasma. Extragalactic jets generate strong shocks in the ambient medium, associated with observed hotspots in FRII radio galaxies, and carve cavities that are filled with the shocked jet flow, dragging a large fraction of the interstellar gas along as slow, massive outflows within the host galaxies. This mechanical output modifies the structural environment (relational output) and deposits energy into the intergalactic medium (energetic output).

**Subsystem O.5 — Radio Lobe Output.** The radio lobe linking to the tail of the jet forms because the material of the jet interacts with the surrounding media. Lobes represent a long-timescale, persistent energetic and relational output — inflated cavities of hot, magnetized plasma that reshape the thermal structure of the host galaxy's gaseous halo.

**Subsystem O.6 — Cosmic Ray Output.** Ultra-high-energy cosmic rays (UHECRs) may be accelerated in blazar jets via diffusive shock acceleration or magnetic reconnection, constituting a particle-based energetic output channel distinct from photonic or neutrino output.

**Mode Assessment for Output:**

O_E: Synchrotron luminosity, IC luminosity, mechanical jet power, neutrino energy flux, cosmic ray energy flux — multiple independent energetic output channels. O_I: Polarization state encodes magnetic field geometry, SED shape encodes particle population, variability patterns encode jet dynamics, neutrino detection encodes hadronic content — multiple informational output channels. ### Element 3: PROCESSING (P) — Multiple Subsystems Identified

The blazar performs energy and information transformation through at least five physically distinct processing mechanisms.

**Subsystem P.1 — Blandford-Znajek (BZ) Mechanism.** Blandford and Znajek proposed that the relativistic jet extracts the rotation energy of the black hole; in this scenario, the jet power depends on the spin and mass of the black hole. This is a specific energy transformation pathway: black hole rotational energy → Poynting flux → jet kinetic energy. It operates via magnetohydrodynamic coupling between the black hole ergosphere and the threading magnetic field.

**Subsystem P.2 — Blandford-Payne (BP) Mechanism.** Blandford and Payne suggested that the jet power is extracted from the rotation energy of accretion disks. This is a parallel, physically distinct processing pathway operating simultaneously with BZ — disk rotational energy → centrifugally driven outflow along magnetic field lines. The two mechanisms may operate in complementary fashion, with research indicating both may contribute to jet formation in both FSRQs and BL Lacs.

**Subsystem P.3 — Particle Acceleration Processing.** Multiple particle acceleration mechanisms operate within the jet. Diffusive acceleration at relativistic shocks is believed to be the main mechanism for energizing electrons and ions, because it is both extremely efficient and very fast, with acceleration rates of the order of the gyrofrequency. Additionally, relativistic magnetic reconnection, a process that involves the rapid reconstruction of magnetic field structure in plasma dominated by magnetic energy, has emerged as a widely applicable and promising mechanism for particle acceleration. These are parallel processing subsystems — shocks and reconnection can operate simultaneously at different locations within the jet.

**Subsystem P.4 — Radiative Processing.** Synchrotron emission and inverse Compton scattering are themselves processing operations that transform particle kinetic energy into photon energy. The synchrotron process converts perpendicular momentum into radiation; the IC process transfers electron energy to photons via scattering. These are energy-form transformations (P_E) that also constitute information processing (P_I) in the sense that the output spectrum encodes the particle distribution function.

**Subsystem P.5 — Accretion Disk Thermal Processing.** For accretion rates above a critical threshold, the flow is well described by a geometrically thin, radiatively efficient disk model. The accretion disk converts gravitational potential energy to thermal radiation via viscous dissipation — a distinct processing pathway from the jet mechanisms.

**Mode Assessment for Processing:**

P_E: BZ extraction, BP extraction, shock acceleration, magnetic reconnection, viscous dissipation — multiple parallel energy transformation pathways. P_I: Flavor oscillation analogy absent, but spectral energy distribution shaping via synchrotron/IC serves as information transformation; particle distribution function evolution constitutes pattern transformation. P_R: A key prediction of MHD models is a transition from poloidally dominated fields near the engine to toroidally dominated fields downstream — this represents relational processing, a transformation of the magnetic field topology (structural reorganization of the field geometry). Magnetic reconnection itself is fundamentally relational processing: the destruction and reformation of magnetic field line connectivity. P_T: Oscillation phase accumulation has no direct analog, but jet acceleration/collimation occurs over extended spatial and temporal scales. ## PART IV: MODE DEFICIENCY ANALYSIS

**Potential T-mode deficiency in young/nascent jets:** Observations suggest a possibly nascent jet embedded in and interacting with a dense medium resulting in jet bending.
