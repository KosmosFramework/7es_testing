## Detailed 7ES Analysis

### Element 1: Input - Multiple Distinct Subsystems Identified

**Primary Input Subsystems:**

1. **Wolf Predation Subsystem**
   - Direct elk mortality (approximately 9,100 elk killed by wolves, 1995-2011)
   - Mechanism: Physical predation events
   - Temporal pattern: Seasonal and opportunistic

2. **Human Hunting Subsystem**
   - Human-caused elk mortality (approximately 16,700 elk killed by humans, 1995-2011)
   - Mechanism: Regulated hunting outside park boundaries
   - Temporal pattern: Seasonal hunting periods

3. **Multi-Predator Subsystem**
   - Additional predation from grizzly bears, black bears, and cougars
   - Mechanism: Diverse predatory behaviors and seasonal patterns
   - Cumulative effect on elk population dynamics

4. **Environmental Energy Subsystem**
   - Solar energy for photosynthesis in aspen
   - Seasonal light cycles affecting growth patterns
   - Mechanism: Photoperiodic responses

5. **Nutritional Resource Subsystem**
   - Elk consumption of aspen biomass
   - Soil nutrients and water for aspen root systems
   - Mechanism: Metabolic and growth processes

**Assessment:** Input exhibits five parallel subsystems operating through distinct mechanisms, confirming multiple pathway functionality.
