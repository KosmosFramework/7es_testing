Relational (R)	Structural connections, constraints, symmetries	How are things related?
Temporal (T)	Time-dependent dynamics, persistence, rhythm	When and how long?
These four modes are orthogonal (each captures a distinct aspect of system behavior) and universal (present in all systems across all domains).
