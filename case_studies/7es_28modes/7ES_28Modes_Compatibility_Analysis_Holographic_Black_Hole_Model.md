This relational input is distinct from both energetic and informational input; it describes how the infalling matter is connected to other systems.

**I_T — Temporal Input:**

The temporal input includes the production time and phase of infalling quanta, the rate of accretion, and the cosmological epoch (which determines the temperature of the cosmic microwave background relative to the Hawking temperature). **Subsystem Assessment for Input:** At minimum three distinct energetic input pathways, one primary informational pathway (quantum state data), one relational pathway (entanglement and constraint structure), and one temporal pathway (timing/rate context). Input is multi-subsystem.
